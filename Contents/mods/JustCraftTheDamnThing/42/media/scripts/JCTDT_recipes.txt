	/*
	========================================================================
	 Just Craft The Damn Thing  --  Build 42 craftRecipe definitions
	 Conventions taken from vanilla recipes_tailoring.txt / recipes_cannedFood.txt:
	 - Sewing:      timedAction = SewingCloth   (needle animation + sound)
	 - Ripping:     timedAction = RipClothing   (rip animation + cloth-tearing sound)
	 - Cans:        explicit item list + itemMapper, plus the vanilla
	                RecipeCodeOnCreate.openCan handler (does the conversion and
	                the vanilla injury / tool-damage roll)
	 - Skills:      MetalWelding and Carving are the exact B42 perk names
	 - Tags:        InHandCraft = shows in the hand menu; AnySurfaceCraft = needs
	                a surface; removing CanBeDoneInDark makes a recipe need light
	 - Shield:      the first craftRecipe in a file can fail to register in B42,
	                so a harmless placeholder recipe is kept first on purpose
	 - Translations: media/lua/shared/Translate/<LANG>/*.json (keys = raw recipe ID)
	========================================================================
	*/
