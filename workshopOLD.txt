description=Adds a small collection of logical, believable crafting recipes missing from vanilla Project Zomboid (Build 42).
description=
description=If it makes sense, you should probably be able to craft it.
description=
description=If you want me to add a specific crafting recipe, please ask in the dedicated discussion.
description=
description=If you want me to add support for a specific language, please check the dedicated discussion.
description=
description=[b]Multiplayer has not been tested, but it should work.[/b]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=
description=This mod has a compatibility patch for [b]Vanilla Foods Expanded[/b] (3577903007).
description=
description=[h2]Optional Sandbox Settings[/h2]
description=
description=You can use Sandbox Settings to disable some or all of the recipes (why would you do that?) if you have [b]Dane's Library[/b] (3715021740) installed.

description=[h1]Recipe List[/h1]
description=
description=[h2]Tailoring[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Sew Sheet from Rags[/b] — 10 Ripped Sheets + Thread + Needle → 1 Sheet
description=[list]
description=[*]The Needle is retained and is not consumed.
description=[*]Requires Tailoring 1.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Rip Underwear[/b] — 1 cloth sock, underwear, or bra → 1 Ripped Sheet
description=[list]
description=[*]The item must not be worn.
description=[/list]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Cooking[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Open Canned Food with Screwdriver[/b] — Canned Food + Screwdriver → Opened Canned Food
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires a light source and a suitable surface.
description=[*]There is a risk of injury and tool breakage.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Open Canned Food with Scissors[/b] — Canned Food + Scissors → Opened Canned Food
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires a light source and a suitable surface.
description=[*]There is a risk of injury and tool breakage.
description=[/list]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Carving[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Carve Long Sticks from Plank[/b] — 1 Plank + Knife → 3 Long Sticks
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires Carving 1.
description=[/list]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Welding[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Weld Improvised Metal Pipe[/b] — 1 Metal Sheet + Hammer + Blowtorch + Welding Mask → 1 Metal Pipe
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires MetalWelding 4.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Weld Improvised Sledgehammer[/b] — 1 Metal Bar + 4 Metal Sheets + Hammer + Blowtorch + Welding Mask → 1 Improvised Sledgehammer
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires MetalWelding 5.
description=[*]The Improvised Sledgehammer is a new item. It works similarly to a regular Sledgehammer, but has significantly lower durability.
description=[/list]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Miscellaneous[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Tear Book into Paper[/b] — 1 Book → 10 Sheets of Paper
description=[list]
description=[*]Works with novels, magazines, newspapers, notebooks, comics, etc.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Break Rabbit Skull into Bone[/b] — 1 Rabbit Skull → 1 Small Bone
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires Strength 1.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Grind Improvised Whetstone[/b] — 3 Stones → 1 Crude Whetstone
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires Maintenance 1.
